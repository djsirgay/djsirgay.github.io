04 / Mission

“DJ Sir Gay” takes a childhood insult built around Sergey / Sir Gay and turns it into identity, authorship and signal.

This is not a generic DJ persona. It is a cultural proposition: queer freedom, Eastern European pop memory and Los Angeles nightlife in one voice. The goal is not to flatten the past into nostalgia — it is to make the past dance differently.

Music becomes a bridge between exile and belonging, between the catalog people grew up with and the freedom they were not always allowed to name.
