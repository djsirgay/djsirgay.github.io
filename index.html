03 / MISSION

Turn the word
against them.

Music is not separate from identity, memory or freedom. It is how a life pushed into exile becomes audible again.

“This is not nostalgia. It is a country carried through sound.”

DJ Sir Gay reclaims an insult built around Sergey / “Sir Gay” and turns it into authorship. Queer freedom, Belarusian and Eastern European pop memory, forced exile and Los Angeles nightlife meet in one voice.

The two-hour Belarus in Exile set moves through homeland, pressure, rupture, escape, exile and release. Fifty-five Belarusian-language tracks become a cultural statement against censorship and the disappearance of artists.
