03 / ARTIST STORY

A taunt became
a name.

DJ Sir Gay is the Los Angeles-based project of queer Belarusian artist in exile Sergey Ulyanov. The name reclaims a childhood taunt and turns it into authorship, self-irony and a public language for freedom.

His signature work, Belarus in Exile — From Dictatorship to the Dancefloor, is a two-hour journey through 55 Belarusian-language tracks. It turns homeland, pressure, rupture, escape and exile into a continuous dancefloor narrative.
